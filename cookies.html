Prototype notice · Ireland

Cookies & storage.

Rigwise's app code does not set cookies. It stores a small set of preferences and saved-build data in this browser using local storage.

Last reviewed 18 November 2026 · Version 1
Consent review still needed. This prototype has not been reviewed for cookie or device-storage consent requirements. Do not treat the current absence of analytics cookies as a legal determination.

Local storage used by the app

  • rigwise-local-account-id-v1 stores the browser-local ID.
  • rigwise-saved-builds-v1:<ID> stores saved builds for that ID.
  • rigwise-county stores the selected county.
  • rigwise-theme stores the light/dark theme choice.

An older saved-build key may be read once and migrated to the current ID key. Local storage stays in the browser until cleared or replaced; it is not a cookie.

Clear it

Use “Clear local data” in the account panel to delete Rigwise local-storage keys and create a new ID on reload. You can also clear this site's storage in your browser settings. Downloaded backup files need to be deleted separately.

Hosting access controls

Vercel may apply its own platform controls to protected preview deployments. Those controls are separate from Rigwise app code. Review the Vercel Privacy Notice for its platform practices.

Policy status

No cookie banner or consent preference is implemented. Whether the current local-storage items qualify for an exception has not been determined. Complete that review before adding analytics, advertising, affiliate tracking or other non-essential storage.
