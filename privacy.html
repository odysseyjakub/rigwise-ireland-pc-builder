Privacy & data

Rigwise stores your builds in this browser. There are no email accounts or cloud sync.

Prototype notice. Part and accessory prices are examples, not retailer offers. The site operator’s legal identity and privacy contact have not been provided. Do not enter personal information.

Saved on this device

Your Rigwise ID, saved builds, theme and questionnaire preferences stay in this browser’s local storage. The ID is a private label for your saved builds, not a login or password.

You can download or restore a backup, copy the ID, or clear Rigwise data from Saved.

Build links

A shared build link contains selected parts and build preferences. It never includes your local Rigwise ID or saved builds. Anyone with the link can see that build configuration.

Performance and product images

When you open FPS results, Rigwise sends the selected CPU, GPU, games and resolutions to its Vercel endpoint, which requests estimates from FPSHQ. The request does not include your local ID, saved builds or address. Product thumbnails may load from the manufacturer or image host named for that part.

Choices and launch status

Rigwise does not include analytics or advertising scripts. You can clear browser data in the Saved section or through your browser’s site-storage settings. A complete commercial privacy notice requires the operator’s identity, contact details and confirmed data-handling terms.
