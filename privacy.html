Who is responsible?

The operator's legal name, postal address and privacy contact have not been provided for this prototype. That information is therefore not published here. A responsible controller and working contact route must be identified before the site is used as a public commercial service.

What the site stores in this browser

Rigwise uses browser local storage for its anonymous ID, saved builds, selected county and theme preference. The ID is a label for this browser's saved builds; it is not a password or sign-in credential. These values are not synced to a server by the app.

  • rigwise-local-account-id-v1: the local ID.
  • rigwise-saved-builds-v1:<ID>: saved builds associated with that ID.
  • rigwise-county and rigwise-theme: the selected county and visual theme.

The app may read the legacy rigwise-demo-builds-v1 key once to migrate earlier saved builds. The “Clear local data” control removes Rigwise local-storage keys from this browser. A downloaded backup is a separate file on your device and is not removed by that control.

Shared builds and backups

A share link encodes the selected component IDs, budget, workload and county in the URL fragment. Anyone who receives the full link can open that build configuration. The build fragment is not a secret or an account credential.

A downloaded backup contains the local ID and saved builds. Restoring it replaces the ID and saved builds in that browser after confirmation. The app does not upload the file.

Files you review

The Awin feed review reads a selected CSV or TSV file in your browser to count exact matches, ambiguous rows and parse failures. It does not upload or persist the file, and it never adds imported prices to the optimiser.

Optional mapped-shop lookup

If you choose “Find mapped shops”, the selected county is sent in a request to this site's Vercel function. The function queries the public Overpass service operated by ovp.dresel.systems for named computer and electronics shops recorded in OpenStreetMap. The request does not send your local account ID, exact address or device location. Successful county results may be cached by Vercel for six hours and served stale for up to one day while a refresh is attempted; the lookup is not saved in browser storage. Vercel and the Overpass operator may process technical request information under their own notices. The Overpass service says it records anonymous aggregate usage counts and provides no availability guarantee. Review the Overpass service notice, its OpenStreetMap attribution and licence information, and Vercel's Privacy Notice.

Hosting and third parties

The site is hosted on Vercel. The app does not include analytics or advertising scripts and no longer requests external font files. Vercel's platform may process technical request information; see the Vercel Privacy Notice. The service-specific controller/processor arrangements and retention period have not been documented in this prototype.

Your choices and rights

You can clear app data with the control in the account panel or through your browser's site-storage settings. A working contact route for requests about hosting-level data has not been supplied; this is a prelaunch gap. See the Irish Data Protection Commission guidance for information about transparency and data-subject rights.
