WHAT A LISTING NEEDS

Evidence before exposure.

01

Exact product

Model and part number match a known component, with a working product page.

02

Real seller and price

Seller identity, VAT treatment, currency and a dated observed price are recorded.

03

Ireland checkout

Delivery eligibility and charges are supported by evidence for the Republic of Ireland.

04

Honest freshness

Stock and last-confirmed time are visible; stale or uncertain offers are not called live.

Public data needs permission and context.

Manufacturer pages help identify hardware, but they are not retailer offers. Public retailer pages, affiliate feeds and marketplace APIs can have different reuse rules and may omit final Irish checkout costs. We’re researching permitted sources; none is enabled in this demo.
