OUTSIDE-EU DISPATCH

Check the dispatch country before assuming the final price.

Revenue says a €3 customs-duty charge per distinct item applies to qualifying e-commerce packages valued at €150 or less from outside the EU from 1 July 2026. A €2 Union Handling Fee per distinct item also applies from 1 November 2026. VAT and any carrier administration charge may be collected at checkout or on delivery. A shop’s domain or currency does not prove where an item ships from.

This form does not calculate outside-EU landed charges or tariff rates. Use the Revenue guidance for the exact dispatch route, customs classification and seller charge treatment before relying on a total.
