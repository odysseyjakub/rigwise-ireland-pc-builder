OUTSIDE-EU DISPATCH

Check the dispatch country before assuming the final price.

Revenue says a €3 customs-duty charge per distinct item applies to qualifying e-commerce packages valued at €150 or less from outside the EU from 1 July 2026. A €2 Union Handling Fee per distinct item applies from 1 November 2026. Revenue describes the handling fee as a charge aimed at platforms and businesses; it affects your cost only if the seller passes it on. VAT and any carrier administration charge may be collected at checkout or on delivery. A shop’s domain or currency does not prove where an item ships from.

The optional local calculator below requires you to enter the relevant customs values and confirm seller charge treatment. It does not look up tariff rates, customs territory, or seller terms for you.
